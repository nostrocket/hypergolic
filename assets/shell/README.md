# Shell artwork

`launch-night.jpg` and `launch-morning.jpg` are the paired Home wallpapers. They
were generated for Hypergolic from the approved night/morning launch concepts,
then exported as opaque JPEGs at quality 90. Together they add about 245 KiB.
They contain only artwork; labels, icons and system controls remain native UI.

Home selects the scene from the OS appearance. The atmospheric image scales to the viewport, keeping its quiet sky and low
horizon in the same proportions across screen shapes instead of zooming into
the bright launch glow in landscape. The wallpaper is static, bundled for offline use, hidden from accessibility
and excluded from touch handling. Other shell scenes retain their layered artwork.

The morning scene uses stronger secondary text and orange links through
`homePalette`, scoped to Home and its header. Keep the calm upper sky and low
horizon when replacing either image; check actual labels on both portrait and
landscape screens, including large text and a populated grid.
